# Privacy Policy — Personal Cable TV Companion

**Effective date:** September 15, 2026

Personal Cable TV Companion is designed to let users view and control their own Personal Cable TV system from a Roku device. The app is intended for personal media libraries that the user already owns or is authorized to access.

## Information the app uses

Personal Cable TV Companion does not require a developer-operated user account and does not require users to submit their name, email address, payment information, or other personal profile information to the developer.

To function, the app may use connection information needed to reach the user's own Personal Cable TV server on the user's network, such as a local server address, device/session identifiers, or access tokens. These settings are used only to connect the Roku app to the user's Personal Cable TV system.

## Media and library data

Personal Cable TV Companion does not upload the user's movies, TV shows, media files, or library contents to a developer-operated cloud service. Playback and guide information are provided by the user's own Personal Cable TV server and related services configured by the user, such as Jellyfin.

## Data collection and selling

The developer does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

The app does not use developer-operated advertising or analytics services to build advertising profiles about users.

## Local network communication

The app is designed primarily to communicate with the user's own Personal Cable TV server over the user's local network. The availability, security, and configuration of that server are controlled by the user.

## Third-party services

The Roku platform, Roku devices, Jellyfin, operating systems, network providers, or other third-party services used with Personal Cable TV Companion may collect information under their own privacy policies. This Privacy Policy applies only to Personal Cable TV Companion and does not control the practices of those third parties.

## Children

Personal Cable TV Companion is a general-purpose personal media application and is not specifically directed to children.

## Security

Users are responsible for securing their own home network, Personal Cable TV server, Jellyfin server, passwords, API keys, access tokens, and media systems. Users should not expose local services to the public internet unless they understand and accept the security risks involved.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The current version will be published in this repository.
